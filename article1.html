GARCH-GNN Hybrid Volatility Forecasting Framework: Architecture Design and Out-of-Sample Backtesting

GARCH-GNN混合波动率预测框架：架构设计与样本外回测

发布时间：2026 | 研究方向：金融计量、波动率建模、图神经网络

Abstract

摘要

Traditional GARCH-type models rely on linear lag structures to capture conditional heteroskedasticity and demonstrate reliable performance under stationary market regimes. However, they struggle with abrupt market jumps, structural breaks, and nonlinear cross-asset contagion effects. This work proposes a hybrid GARCH-GNN framework that combines classical econometric volatility dynamics with graph-based nonlinear feature learning.

传统GARCH类模型依靠线性滞后结构捕捉条件异方差，在平稳市场环境中表现稳定，但难以应对市场突变、结构断裂与跨资产非线性风险传染。本文构建GARCH-GNN混合研究框架，融合经典计量波动动力学与图网络非线性特征学习能力。

1. Introduction

1. 研究概述

Financial time series are widely characterized by volatility clustering, heavy tails, leverage effects and non-stationarity. GARCH family models have long become the standard tool in volatility econometrics due to their solid statistical foundation.

金融时间序列普遍存在波动集聚、厚尾分布、杠杆效应与非平稳特性，GARCH体系凭借扎实的统计基础成为波动率计量主流工具。

2. Model Architecture

2. 模型架构

  • Univariate GARCH(p,q) conditional variance recursion
  • High-frequency feature engineering
  • Dynamic graph construction based on time-varying correlation
  • Graph convolution layers for cross-asset risk embedding
  • 单资产GARCH(p,q)条件方差迭代运算
  • 高频金融因子特征工程构建
  • 基于时变相关性的动态图结构搭建
  • 图卷积层实现跨资产风险特征嵌入

Conclusion

研究结论

The integration of econometric modeling and graph deep learning provides a feasible path to reconcile statistical interpretability and nonlinear predictive power.

计量模型与图深度学习融合，有效兼顾统计可解释性与非线性预测能力，大幅提升波动率预测稳健性。
