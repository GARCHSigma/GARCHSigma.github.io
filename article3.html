Volatility Clustering and Cross-Market Risk Spillover: Empirical Evidence from Global Asset Classes

波动率集聚与跨市场风险溢出：全球大类资产实证研究

Published: 2026 | Research: Cross-Asset Econometrics, Risk Transmission, Volatility Linkage
发布时间：2026 | 研究方向：跨资产计量、风险传导、波动联动性

Abstract

摘要

With deepening global capital market integration, volatility shocks in single markets rapidly spread across different assets and regions, forming obvious cross-border risk spillover effects.

随着全球资本市场一体化程度加深，单一市场波动冲击会快速传导至各类资产与不同区域市场，形成显著的跨市场风险溢出效应。

1. Research Background

1. 研究背景

Volatility clustering is the most typical stylized fact in financial time series. Risk events trigger capital reallocation and sentiment resonance, breaking independent fluctuation rules of traditional single markets.

波动集聚是金融时间序列最典型的特征事实，风险事件会引发资金再配置与市场情绪共振，打破传统单一市场独立运行规律。

2. Research Methods

2. 研究方法

  • Realized volatility calculation based on high-frequency transaction data
  • Dynamic volatility spillover index construction
  • Time-varying correlation measurement between multi-class assets
  • 基于高频交易数据构建已实现波动率序列
  • 动态波动率溢出指数搭建与趋势追踪
  • 多品类资产时变联动相关性量化测算

Core Findings

核心结论

Global equity markets act as major risk exporters, while commodities and foreign exchange markets are sensitive risk receivers. Spillover intensity rises sharply during financial stress periods.

全球权益市场大多周期内为风险输出端，商品与外汇市场属于高敏感风险接收端，金融压力区间内风险溢出强度会出现急剧抬升。
