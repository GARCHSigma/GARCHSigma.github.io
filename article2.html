Gamma Exposure Mechanism: How Options GEX Drives Short-Term Equity Market Volatility

伽马敞口机制：期权GEX如何驱动短期市场波动

Published: 2026 | Research: Market Microstructure, Options Flow, Volatility Dynamics
发布时间：2026 | 研究方向：市场微观结构、期权资金流、波动动力学

Abstract

摘要

Options gamma exposure has become one of the most influential short-term driving factors in modern equity markets. Dealer hedging behavior triggered by net gamma position significantly amplifies intraday price fluctuation and forms a unique volatility feedback loop.

期权伽马敞口已成为当代股票市场最核心的短期影响因子之一，做市商基于净伽马头寸产生的对冲行为会显著放大日内价格波动，形成特殊的波动反馈循环。

1. Theoretical Foundation

1. 理论基础

Gamma reflects the change rate of option delta. Negative gamma environment forces market makers to sell in decline and buy in rise, which aggravates market volatility. Positive gamma can effectively stabilize intraday fluctuation range.

伽马代表期权Delta变动速率，市场处于负伽马环境时，做市商跌时抛售、涨时买入，加剧行情震荡；正伽马环境则能够有效收敛日内波动幅度。

2. Transmission Path

2. 传导路径

  • Aggregated institutional and retail option positions shape overall market gamma level
  • Dynamic delta hedging generates synchronous directional trading flows
  • Hedging capital flows disturb original market supply and demand balance
  • 机构与散户期权持仓汇总形成全域市场伽马水平
  • 动态Delta对冲催生同向集中交易资金流
  • 对冲资金流打破原生市场供需均衡状态

Conclusion

研究结论

GEX indicators can be combined with traditional volatility models to optimize short-term volatility interval forecasting and improve dynamic risk management efficiency.

伽马敞口指标可结合传统波动率模型优化短期波动区间预判，提升系统化策略动态风控效率。
