About

关于本站

GARCHSigma focuses on advanced financial econometrics, high-order volatility modeling, and nonlinear time-series analysis. The research scope primarily covers generalized autoregressive conditional heteroskedasticity frameworks, high-frequency market econometrics, cross-asset risk structure identification, and systematic quantitative modeling.

GARCHSigma 专注于高阶金融计量经济学、波动率建模与非线性时间序列分析，研究范畴涵盖广义自回归条件异方差体系、高频市场计量、跨资产风险结构识别与系统化量化建模框架。

This website serves as an academic archive for theoretical derivations, model implementations, empirical validations, and market cycle observations. All content is strictly for academic research and econometric verification, and does not constitute any financial investment advice.

本站为学术研究存档平台，用于收录理论推导、模型实现、实证检验与市场周期观测内容。所有内容仅限学术计量研究使用，不构成任何投资建议。

Research Fields

核心研究方向

  • Extended GARCH-type models, multivariate stochastic volatility, and long-horizon volatility forecasting
  • Graph neural networks and deep learning applications for non-stationary financial time series
  • Cross-asset linkage dynamics, options gamma exposure, and market microstructure liquidity behavior
  • Systematic quantitative framework design, risk parity allocation, tail risk hedging, and drawdown constraint modeling
  • Macroeconomic cycle analysis, sentiment pricing, and long-term volatility premium empirical studies
  • GARCH拓展模型、多元随机波动率建模与长周期波动率预测
  • 图神经网络与深度学习在非平稳金融时序中的计量应用
  • 跨资产联动机制、期权伽马敞口与市场微观流动性研究
  • 系统化量化框架搭建、风险平价配置、尾部风险对冲与回撤约束设计
  • 宏观周期研判、情绪定价与波动率溢价长周期实证分析
